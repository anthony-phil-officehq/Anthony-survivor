# About these docs

Hello, I'm Anthony Phil (Phuoc Nguyen).

These docs collect what I have learned about the OfficeHQ system, through my own research and through knowledge-transfer sessions. They are **not official documentation**, and they do not come from sources such as source code docs or the organisation's Confluence. They reflect my personal understanding of the system.

All pages are created by AI agents. Before I publish a page, I review it and make sure its content fully matches my own understanding of the system.

## Why this site exists

- **One place for what I know.** It brings together my knowledge, lessons learned, and best practices.
- **A central reference.** It supports research and quick lookups, so I don't have to search several sources such as the codebase and Confluence.
