## Communication channels

### Microsoft Teams

**Dialled Up Standup & Afterparty** — daily meeting, **Monday / Tuesday / Thursday / Friday, 8:30–9:00 AM**.

- **Join:** [Microsoft Teams meeting](https://teams.microsoft.com/meet/49306462409910?p=oxLZwrTHuXbSVa3pqH)
- **Purpose:** The development team informs each other about current work with regards to the sprint goal. This coordination ensures everyone has a good view of progress.
- **Structure:** Each team member answers:
  1. What I did yesterday?
  2. What am I planning to do today?
  3. Blockers?

  Discussion is kept short; lengthier topics move to the "afterparty" that follows.
